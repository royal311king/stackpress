# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Homelab operators and technically confident WordPress owners managing Docker-based sites, mounted storage, backups, and restores across local machines.

## Product Purpose

StackPress is a self-hosted control panel that makes WordPress backup, restore, health, and scheduling operations reliable and visible. Success means infrastructure can move between disks or machines without repetitive site-by-site reconfiguration.

## Brand Personality

Serious, direct, dependable. The interface should create operational confidence without hiding technical details.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative SaaS dashboards, novelty controls, vague status language, hidden paths, and workflows that make routine infrastructure changes repetitive or fragile.

## Design Principles

- Make system state and resolved paths explicit.
- Centralize shared infrastructure configuration.
- Use progressive disclosure for exceptional overrides.
- Prefer safe automation with clear recovery paths.
- Keep power-user workflows fast without punishing occasional operators.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard access. Never communicate status by color alone, honor reduced-motion preferences, and keep technical values readable and copyable.
